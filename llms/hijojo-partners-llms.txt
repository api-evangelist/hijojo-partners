# HiJoJo Partners

> HiJoJo Partners Inc. (HiJoJo Partners株式会社) is a Tokyo-based independent asset management
> and fintech group, established November 2017, registered with the Director of the Kanto Local
> Finance Bureau (No. 3065) for Type II Financial Instruments Business, Investment Management
> Business, and Investment Advisory and Agency Business. It forms, sells and manages funds that
> give Japanese institutional and individual investors access to late-stage private and pre-IPO
> companies, and works to develop Japan's secondary market for unlisted shares.
